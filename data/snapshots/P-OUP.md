# Policy on AI Use & Disclosure for OUP Journals Authors

This policy explains how authors may use generative AI tools (Gen AI Tools) when preparing manuscripts for OUP journals, and when disclosure is required. This guidance is based on [our AI Principles for OUP Journals](https://academic.oup.com/pages/for-authors/journals/preparing-and-submitting-your-manuscript/ai-principles-for-oxford-journals) and applies to all content types.

Please note that this policy outlines the core expectations for authors. However, individual journals may have custom requirements, in addition to those stated here, to best serve the needs of their specific research communities. Authors should consult the author guidelines of their chosen journal prior to submission to ensure they are in compliance with all journal policies. In the event of any variation between this policy and the journal’s specific guidance, authors should follow the journal’s specific guidance.

You will be required to assert upon submission of your article in your journal’s submission system that you have read and adhered to this policy and any journal specific policy variations.

### Permissible uses of Gen AI Tools & disclosure requirements

To help authors when considering where and how to define and describe use of AI when disclosing, please see the [STM Recommendations for a Classification of AI Use](https://s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/stm.offloadmedia/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/23020709/STM_AI_Classification_Recs_19_Sept2025-1.pdf). These Recommendations have been created with input from voices across scholarly publishing and are intended to cover all disciplines.

Disclosure is essential to ensure that AI-generated content is not presented as entirely human-created. Authors are accountable for complete and accurate disclosure.

### Uses of Gen AI Tools permitted WITHOUT disclosure

- Improving clarity of language (e.g. spelling or grammar correction)
- Formatting references to comply with journal style requirements (authors must ensure that all references are accurate and valid with no hallucinations)

### Uses of Gen AI Tools which are typically permitted WITH disclosure

- Substantially editing, rewriting, or reorganizing text using AI
- Translating text using AI
- Collecting, organizing, formatting, analyzing, and/or visualizing data using AI
- Generating or refining code using AI
- Generating illustrative or explanatory images, such as graphical abstracts, using AI
- Manipulating images or figures from published works, or creating derivative versions of them, provided that requirements relating to authorship, disclosure, use of third-party materials, and accountability are met

### Uses of Gen AI Tools NOT permitted

- Creating content by Gen AI Tools and misrepresenting as though it is original research data/results created by humans
- Creating or altering primary factual, scientific, or technical images, such as clinical or diagnostic images, documentary images, or images of real people, objects, or events

### Where to disclose AI use

Any use of Gen AI Tools should be disclosed both in your cover letter to editors and in the Acknowledgements section of manuscripts. You may also be required to disclose specific uses in the Methodology section of your manuscript where the use of Gen AI Tools was integral to your research methodology. If you are uncertain, please refer to the individual journal Author Guidelines or editorial office for clarification. This disclosure must be made at initial submission and updated as relevant for any revision(s) submitted.

### How to disclose AI use

When disclosing AI use in the cover letter and manuscript, the following details must be included:

- Name and version of the Gen AI Tool used
- A description of how the Gen AI Tool was used (e.g. substantial editing or text generation, organization of document, translation of manuscript text, etc.)
- The author’s role in directing and reviewing the work generated by the Gen AI Tool to ensure accuracy of the output

For more complex use cases, or in cases where the editorial team may require additional information, the following details are strongly recommended to be included:

- Date(s) of use
- Specific sections of the document where the Gen AI Tool was used

### If you are unsure about something related to AI use or disclosure

Please visit the journal’s Author Guidelines page to check for journal specific exceptions to the policy and/or contact the editorial office before submission.

### Consequences of non-disclosure

Each journal retains editorial discretion in determining whether submitted content is appropriate for publication. Transparent disclosure of AI use supports informed editorial judgment and contributes to the integrity and reliability of the scholarly record.

Pre-publication: where disclosed use of Gen AI Tools does not align with journal policy, authors will be informed of the next steps, which may include being asked to revise their manuscript accordingly. Failure to disclose use of Gen AI Tools may be grounds for rejection.

Post publication: journals and their editorial teams can decide to take action to correct the accuracy of the literature in accordance with industry standards such as the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). These actions can include corrections or in some cases retractions to address any form of misrepresentation, including the undisclosed involvement of Gen AI Tools.
