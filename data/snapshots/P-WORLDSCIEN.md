# World Scientific Policy on AI and Authorship

World Scientific recognizes that the use of artificial intelligence tools (AI) in academic research and writing is an evolving practice. AI-based tools and technologies include but are not limited to large language models (LLMs), generative AI, and chatbots (for example, ChatGPT).

World Scientific distinguishes between assistive AI, such as tools used for routine spelling, grammar, or language correction, and generative AI, such as tools used to generate or substantially modify text, code, images, or other content, or to assist with data processing or analysis.

Authors remain fully responsible for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and ethical conduct of their work, including any content, analysis, code, images, references, or other material produced or assisted by AI tools.

In choosing to use AI tools, authors are expected to do so responsibly and in accordance with our editorial policies on authorship and principles of publishing ethics.

World Scientific joins [COPE](https://publicationethics.org/cope-position-statements/ai-author) to state that AI tools cannot be listed as authors or co-authors of a work. AI tools cannot satisfy the responsibilities of authorship, including taking responsibility for the work, approving the final version, and responding to questions concerning its accuracy and integrity.

Authors remain responsible for critically evaluating and independently verifying AI-generated outputs. In particular, authors should verify the accuracy of AI-generated factual claims, references, quotations, data, code, and other substantive content.

##### Disclosure Framework

1. **Assistive AI**

The use of AI tools solely for routine copyediting, spelling, grammar, or readability improvements that do not alter the intellectual or substantive content of the work does not require mandatory disclosure.

Where AI is used for substantive translation, interpretation, rewriting, or other purposes that may affect the intellectual or scientific content of the work, the use should be disclosed.


2. **Generative AI**

Authors who use AI tools to generate or substantially modify text, images or graphical elements, or software code, or to collect or analyze data must explicitly declare this use.

The disclosure should identify the AI tool used and describe its purpose and the extent of its use. Disclosure of AI use does not, by itself, imply that such use is permissible. Authors must comply with all applicable publication-specific requirements, research ethics requirements, and publishing policies.


##### Standardized Declaration Format

For journal articles, where disclosure is required, authors should include a dedicated section titled “Declaration of AI Use”, normally immediately preceding the Acknowledgements or References, unless the journal specifies otherwise. For books and book chapters, the declaration should be included in an appropriate and clearly identifiable location within the work, unless the publication specifies otherwise.

A suitable declaration is:

“During the preparation of this work, the author(s) used \[Tool Name, Version\] to \[describe the specific purpose and extent of use, e.g., assist with data analysis or prepare a figure\]. The authors reviewed, verified, and edited the output and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.”

The use of AI tools must comply with the policies and requirements of the relevant World Scientific publication. Disclosure of AI use does not guarantee that the use will be considered appropriate or permissible.

The final decision regarding whether particular AI-generated or AI-assisted content or processes are appropriate for a submitted work rests with the journal's editor or other party responsible for the publication's editorial policy. Such decisions will be made in accordance with the journal's requirements and World Scientific's standards for publication integrity, research ethics, and responsible scholarly communication.

Authors should also ensure that their use of AI tools respects confidentiality, privacy, copyright, and intellectual property rights.
