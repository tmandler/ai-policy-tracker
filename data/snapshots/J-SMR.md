## Author Guidelines

MIT Sloan Management Review will cease print publication following the magazine’s fall issue. Our regular digital content will continue through November 2026.

This winter, MIT Sloan will launch a new, fully digital version of MIT Sloan Management Review that will provide readers with timely, research-based insights from MIT faculty and researchers — as well as other leading management experts outside of MIT.

Authors interested in writing for this new publication may inquire at [mitsmrsubmit@mit.edu](mailto:mitsmrsubmit@mit.edu).
