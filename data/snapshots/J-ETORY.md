Article Processing
Cambridge University Press may use in-house or third-party tools to help process manuscripts and prepare them for publication. Any such work is conducted in accordance with appropriate local privacy and processing laws, applicable Terms of Use, and ethical guidance. For the avoidance of doubt, any in-house or third-party tools deployed by Cambridge or our suppliers that make use of AI will not use submission data or manuscript content to train their models. Where AI and other tools are used in the editorial process between submission and acceptance, editorial decisions will be made by a human who remains responsible for assessing the scholarly and ethical acceptability of your work. In cases of alleged or suspected misconduct, the journal will investigate in line with COPE recommendations.

Manuscripts that were previously submitted to 
Editorial Express
 should be resubmitted via 
https://editorialexpress.com/et

Effective January 28, 2026, new submissions should be made electronically on 
ScholarOne
 via 
https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/econ-theory
. 

Manuscripts must be provided in PDF format with all fonts, tables and graphics embedded. The first page (title page) must list the authors' information, including their affiliations and contact details. A cover letter is required only if the authors need to convey information pertinent to the review—for instance, if the paper was previously submitted to another journal and they wish to share the prior reviews.

Manuscripts are accepted for review on the understanding that the same work has not been and will not be published nor is presently submitted elsewhere. While under editorial review, it is the responsibility of the authors to keep the handling EIC informed about submissions, publication plans, and actual publication of related research or abstracts thereof in other outlets, including letters, journals, review publications, journals in other disciplines, conference proceedings, and published dissertations. It is further understood that all persons listed as authors have given their approval for the submission of the article and that any person cited as a source of personal communication has approved such citation; written authorization may be required at the journal manager’s discretion. Authors are responsible for obtaining written permission to publish material for which they do not own the copyright. Articles and other material published in 
Econometric Theory 
represent the opinions of the authors and should not be construed to reflect the opinions of the Advisory Board, the Editorial Board, or the Publisher
